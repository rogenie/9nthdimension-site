About 9nth Dimension

Last updated November 11, 2026

9nth Dimension teaches the parts of the Bible and church history most churches skip, with the references, so you can go deeper in your faith instead of out of it. Lost and non-canonical texts, how the Bible was assembled, prophecy and the strange, under-taught passages of the Old Testament: told straight, and always fair to the evidence.

Who this is for

You grew up in church and got the Sunday-school version. You're not walking away from your faith; you want more of it. You know your Bible well enough to be bored by surface-level content, but nobody ever told you about Enoch, the Dead Sea Scrolls or how the canon was decided. This is for you.

How we write

  • Scripture first. Every verse we cite is real and correctly referenced, so you can check it yourself.
  • Honest labels. Non-canonical texts like 1 Enoch, Jubilees and the Gospel of Thomas are always labelled as such, along with which traditions accept which books.
  • Debates presented as debates. Where scholars and believers disagree, we give the major readings fairly, including skeptical ones.
  • No sensational claims. We never claim something "proves" the Bible, never set prophetic dates, and never name a living person in prophecy.
  • Kept current. The date at the top of each page shows when it was last reviewed.

The Spiritual Ladder

Knowledge is only the start. Every article sits on one rung of the Spiritual Ladder, a six-stage map of growth from Awakening to Abiding, and ends with a practice you can do today.
