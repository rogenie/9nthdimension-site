**Purpose is the stage where spiritual growth turns outward: you begin using your gifts, story and healing to serve other people.** Peter writes that each person should use whatever gift they have received to serve others (1 Peter 4:10). Purpose is less about a grand title and more about faithful use of what you already have.

## Signs you are on this rung

- You feel drawn to help people who are where you once were.
- You keep asking "what am I meant to do with my life?"
- Certain needs move you more than others, and you cannot ignore them.
- You notice others coming to you for encouragement or advice.

## How to discern your calling

- **Gifts:** what do people consistently thank you for?
- **Burden:** what problem or group of people breaks your heart?
- **Open doors:** where do opportunities keep appearing without force?
- **Peace:** which path brings steady peace, even when it is hard?

Where those four overlap, you have a strong clue to your calling.

## The common trap on this rung

The trap is waiting for a perfect, final calling before you serve at all. Purpose is usually discovered by serving in small ways and paying attention, not by thinking your way to an answer.

## How you know you are ready for Rung 6

You are ready to move up when service flows from rest rather than striving, and peace holds even when results do not. That is Rung 6: Abiding.
