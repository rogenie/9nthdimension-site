**Practice is the stage where faith moves from your quiet time into your choices: how you speak, spend, work and treat people.** James puts it plainly: do not merely listen to the word; do what it says (James 1:22).

## Signs you are on this rung

- Your daily habits are steady, and you want them to change your behaviour.
- You notice a gap between what you believe and how you act under pressure.
- You are drawn to disciplines like fasting, scripture memory or serving.
- You want to set spiritual intentions for your week, not just your morning.

## What practice looks like

- **Intention:** choosing one virtue to practice each week, such as patience, generosity or honesty.
- **Discipline:** keeping a commitment when it is inconvenient, such as a weekly fast or a phone-free evening.
- **Consistency:** returning after you slip, without drama or self-punishment.

## The common trap on this rung

The trap is turning practice into performance: keeping disciplines to feel superior or to earn approval. Hebrews describes mature people as those whose senses are "trained by practice" to tell good from evil (Hebrews 5:14). The point of practice is training, not scorekeeping.

**Today's practice: one virtue, seven days.** Pick one virtue for this week. Each morning, ask for help with it. Each evening, note one moment you practiced it and one moment you missed it. No judgment, just noticing.
</div>

## How you know you are ready for Rung 4

You are ready to move up when your practice starts surfacing old wounds, resentments or patterns you cannot fix by effort alone. That is the doorway to Rung 4: Healing.
