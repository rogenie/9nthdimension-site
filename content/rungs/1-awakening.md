**Awakening is the first stage of spiritual growth: a growing sense that life holds more than what you can see, along with honest questions you can no longer ignore.** It rarely arrives as a bright light. More often it feels like restlessness, a hunger you cannot name, or a moment that cracks your old certainty open.

Ecclesiastes 3:11 says God has set eternity in the human heart. Awakening is the moment you start to notice it.

## Signs you are on this rung

- Things that used to satisfy you now feel thin, even when life is going well.
- You find yourself asking "why am I here?" or "is this all there is?"
- A loss, a crisis or a beautiful moment left you sensing something larger.
- You are curious about prayer, faith or meditation, but unsure where to start.
- You feel pulled toward spiritual content, then doubt whether it is real.

## What is really happening

Awakening is not confusion; it is attention. The questions you are asking are the beginning of a relationship, not a sign that something is wrong with you. Many people stay here for years because they think they need answers before they can begin. You do not. Jesus put it simply: ask, seek, knock (Matthew 7:7). Asking is the first act of faith.

## The common trap on this rung

The trap is consuming instead of practicing. It is easy to watch hours of spiritual videos and still never pray a single honest prayer. Information can wake you up, but only practice moves you up.

<div class="practice" markdown="1">
**Today's practice: the honest question.** Sit somewhere quiet for three minutes. Say one honest question out loud to God, exactly as you feel it, even if it is "are you even there?" Then sit in silence for the rest of the time. Write down anything you notice, even if it is nothing. Do this for seven days.
</div>

## How you know you are ready for Rung 2

You are ready to move up when your curiosity becomes a desire for daily connection, when you want a habit rather than another answer. That is the doorway to Rung 2: Foundations.
