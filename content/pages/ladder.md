**The Spiritual Ladder is a six-stage map of spiritual growth: Awakening, Foundations, Practice, Healing, Purpose and Abiding.** Each rung has its own signs, its own common trap and one simple practice that helps you take the next step. It is a guide, not a scorecard: real growth is rarely a straight line, and most people move up and down the ladder over a lifetime.

## How to find your rung

Read the short descriptions below and notice which one sounds most like your life right now, not the one you wish described you. Then open that rung and start with its practice.

- **If you are full of questions** and unsure where to begin, start at Rung 1: Awakening.
- **If you want a daily habit** that actually lasts, go to Rung 2: Foundations.
- **If your habits are steady** but your faith isn't changing how you live, go to Rung 3: Practice.
- **If old hurts keep surfacing** or prayer feels dry, go to Rung 4: Healing.
- **If you want to know what you're for,** go to Rung 5: Purpose.
- **If you want peace that doesn't depend on circumstances,** go to Rung 6: Abiding.
